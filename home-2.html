Clinical Methodology

Our 5-Stage Precision Medical Pathway

Every medical case follows a structured clinical trajectory designed for diagnostic rigor, surgical safety, and uninterrupted patient telemetry.

1
Pre-Clinical Triage

Vital signs review, rectal temperature, pulse oximetry baseline, body condition score, and computerized history intake.

2
Diagnostic Workup

Point-of-care 15-min chemistry/CBC, digital X-rays, ultrasound screening, or cytology to establish objective clinical markers.

3
Case Formulation

Clinicians review objective findings, establish staging/prognosis, and provide itemized estimates with clear options.

4
Precision Care

Execution of surgical intervention, targeted pharmacological therapy, or ICU stabilization under continuous telemetry.

5
Discharge & Telemetry

Itemized discharge packet, prescribed pharmaceuticals with digital refill tracking, and portal doctor check-ins.
