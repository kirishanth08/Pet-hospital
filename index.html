Designed for Comfort

Hospital Amenities Created for You & Your Pet

We replaced sterile hospital tension with welcoming homestyle hospitality. Every corner of our clinic is engineered for peace of mind.

Separate Dog & Cat Lounges

Separate entrances and quiet feline bays eliminate barking noise and eye contact so cats remain completely relaxed.

Parent Coffee & Beverage Bar

Enjoy fresh artisan coffee, organic herbal teas, sparkling water, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi while you visit.

Private Family Comfort Rooms

Warmly decorated suites with cozy sofas, soft rugs, and dimmable lighting where anxious pets can roam freely.

Calming Pheromones & Music

Hospital-wide Adaptil and Feliway calming pheromone diffusers paired with bio-acoustic music proven to soothe animal heart rates.

Outdoor Relief & Sniff Garden

A secure, fenced grassy relief area with clean waste stations and fresh water bowls so dogs can decompress before stepping inside.

24/7 Pet Parent Digital Portal

Instant online access to view vaccination certificates, chat with your veterinary team, request refills, and track health history.
