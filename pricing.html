Hospital Transparency

Hospital Billing & Treatment FAQs

Answers to common questions regarding written surgical estimates, insurance claims, emergency admissions, and payment policies.

Before any surgical procedure, advanced imaging, or hospital admission, our medical team presents an itemized written estimate outlining the anticipated low and high range of costs. This includes surgical theatre fees, anesthesia, dedicated technician monitoring, surgical supplies, and immediate recovery analgesia. We will never perform non-emergency procedures without your prior written authorization.

Yes! We offer direct pay integration with major pet health insurance carriers including Trupanion, Nationwide, Healthy Paws, and Lemonade. For eligible claims, the insurer pays their portion directly to our hospital at discharge, so you only pay your deductible and copay out-of-pocket rather than paying the full bill upfront.

Our surgical pricing incorporates full AAHA hospital safety protocols: pre-operative blood screening, IV catheterization, continuous balanced fluid infusion, a dedicated 1:1 Licensed Veterinary Technician (LVT) anesthesia nurse, multiparameter vitals telemetry (ECG, SpO2, ETCO2, blood pressure, core body temperature), and 24 hours of tailored multi-modal pain medication.

Emergency admissions begin with a standardized triage and medical examination fee ($145). If hospitalization, oxygen supplementation, blood transfusion, or critical telemetry is indicated, an inpatient admission deposit based on a tailored medical estimate is established upon admission, with daily transparent financial updates provided by your attending critical care veterinarian.
